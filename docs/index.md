# Reading Dubai housing data with dataHabibi

Dubai's #1 housing forecast platform.

## What dataHabibi provides

Dubai's #1 housing forecast platform: price forecasts, rental yields, ROI analysis, and official DLD transaction records for every tower, project, and community in Dubai.

## Explore buildings and communities

Use building-level prices, rental yields and transaction records to research Dubai towers, projects and communities. Compare the context for each property rather than treating a single transaction as the whole market.

## AI-powered property research

dataHabibi is AI-powered: its models read every registered DLD transaction daily to price 5,500+ Dubai buildings, score rental yields, and forecast prices, and Ask AI takes property searches in plain English.
